# Infosys Ltd (INFY) — Q0 Investment Thesis (Q1 FY25)

**Knowledge cutoff:** 2024-07-18 (filing date). **Basis:** Consolidated, Ind AS, ₹ crore.
**Method:** Independent generation by Claude Opus 5 medium and GPT 5.6 Sol high from the
cross-verified public facts; convergent points adopted as the core; Opus's verifiable
computations folded in; product owner (PavanMV) adjudicated and approved 2026-08-21.

Tags: **[FACT]** disclosed; **[INFERENCE]** derived/reasoned; **[SPECULATION]** forward view.

## 1. Thesis (core view)

Constructive but conditional. Q1 FY25 revenue of ₹39,315 cr improved directionally versus
both Q4 FY24 (₹37,923 cr) and Q1 FY24 (₹37,933 cr) — ~3.7% reported rupee growth QoQ and YoY
**[INFERENCE]** — while operating margin held at ~20.8% **[INFERENCE]**, the low end of the
guided 20–22% band. PAT ₹6,374 cr and EPS ₹15.38 confirm solid profitability **[FACT]**. This
is a margin-anchored, delivery-consistency hold — not a growth re-rating: the FY25 case rests
on management's 3–4% constant-currency growth and 20–22% margin guidance **[FACT]** actually
materializing, not on a demand inflection that this quarter does not yet evidence **[SPECULATION]**.

## 2. Key assumptions

- Growth is guided, not demonstrated: 3–4% CC FY25 target **[FACT]** implies acceleration off a
  soft Q1 base; reported ₹ revenue ≠ constant-currency growth, and Q1 CC growth is not disclosed **[INFERENCE]**.
- Margin holds inside 20–22%: Q1 ~20.8% sits at the floor, so reaching mid/upper band needs
  incremental levers (utilization, pyramid, pricing) **[INFERENCE/SPECULATION]**.
- Non-operating income is real but not the engine: total income exceeds revenue by ₹838 cr **[FACT]**;
  treated as treasury/other income supporting PBT, not core-demand quality **[INFERENCE]**.
- Tax is normalized: PBT ₹9,021 cr → PAT ₹6,374 cr implies ~29.3% effective tax **[INFERENCE]**;
  assumed no undisclosed one-off **[INFERENCE]**.
- Base effects flatter optics: flat FY24 quarters make low-single-digit Q1 growth read as a restart **[INFERENCE]**.

## 3. Management commitments to track (checkable next quarter)

- FY25 revenue growth **3–4% constant currency** — reaffirmed, raised, or cut at Q2? **[FACT — commitment]**
- FY25 operating margin **20–22%** — band held, and where within it does Q2 land vs Q1's ~20.8%? **[FACT — commitment]**
- Sequential CC acceleration — later quarters must out-grow Q1 for 3–4% CC to hold **[INFERENCE]**.
- Margin trajectory off the ~20.8% floor toward 21%+ **[INFERENCE]**.
- Whether reported moves reflect operating performance vs currency vs non-operating items **[INFERENCE]**.

## 4. Risks

- Guidance is back-half loaded: no Q2 CC acceleration → steeper H2 → cut risk **[INFERENCE]**.
- Margin at the floor: little cushion; wage revisions, lower utilization, or pricing pressure could breach 20% **[INFERENCE/SPECULATION]**.
- Reported-vs-CC ambiguity: demand quality unverifiable from these facts (no CC, no segment/geo detail) **[FACT — data gap]**.
- Non-operating dependence: the ₹838 cr gap means a treasury swing hits PAT without any core-demand change **[INFERENCE]**.
- Macro/discretionary demand deterioration threatens the guided restart **[SPECULATION]**.

## 5. Observable falsifiers (would break the thesis)

- FY25 CC growth guidance cut below 3% at any FY25 quarter.
- Operating margin printing below 20% in any FY25 quarter, or the 20–22% band withdrawn.
- Q2 FY25 CC sequential growth flat or negative (invalidates the required acceleration).
- Reported revenue declining QoQ in Q2 (below ₹39,315 cr) absent a disclosed currency/scope reason.
- Revenue rising while operating profit/PBT deteriorate materially (growth not converting to earnings),
  or a one-off revealed to have flattered Q1 PAT.

## 6. Open questions

- Q1 FY25 constant-currency growth (QoQ and YoY) vs the ~3.7% reported ₹ figures?
- Segment/vertical/geography mix; discretionary vs cost-takeout split?
- Large-deal TCV and book-to-bill (leading indicator for 3–4% CC deliverability)?
- Headcount, utilization, attrition (the mechanics of holding/expanding margin off the floor)?
- Composition and sustainability of the ₹838 cr non-operating income?
- FY25 capital-return posture (buyback/dividend)?

## 7. Confidence (both models concurred on every rating)

- §1 Thesis — **MEDIUM**: direction well supported; constructive tilt leans on guidance Q1 doesn't yet prove.
- §2 Assumptions — **MEDIUM**: arithmetic solid; forward assumptions reasoned, not disclosed.
- §3 Commitments — **HIGH**: the 3–4% CC and 20–22% targets are explicitly stated and cleanly checkable.
- §4 Risks — **MEDIUM**: follow logically from the numbers/gaps; magnitude unquantifiable from one print.
- §5 Falsifiers — **HIGH**: each concrete, observable, tied to a disclosed metric or its next-quarter successor.

**Central honest caveat (both models surfaced independently):** the facts contain reported rupee
revenue but no Q1 constant-currency growth, while guidance is in CC — so this single quarter cannot,
from these facts alone, confirm the guidance is on track. Reflected in the assumptions, risks, and
open questions rather than papered over.
