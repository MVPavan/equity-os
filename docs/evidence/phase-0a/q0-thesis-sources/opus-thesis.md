# Infosys Ltd (INFY) — Investment Thesis, Post Q1 FY25

**As of the Q1 FY25 print (quarter ended 30 Jun 2024, announced 18 Jul 2024). Knowledge cutoff: 2024-07-18. Consolidated, Ind AS, ₹ crore.**

Tags used below: **[FACT]** = disclosed figure/guidance from the provided set; **[INFERENCE]** = derived arithmetic or reasoned read of the facts; **[SPECULATION]** = forward view not supported by a disclosed number.

---

## 1. THESIS (core view)

Infosys entered FY25 with a modest but positive growth restart — Q1 revenue of ₹39,315 cr grew ~3.7% both QoQ and YoY in reported rupee terms **[INFERENCE]** — while holding operating margin at the low end of its own band (~20.8% derived) **[FACT/INFERENCE]**. The stock is a defensive, margin-anchored compounder here rather than a growth re-rating story: the entire FY25 case rests on management's 3–4% constant-currency growth and 20–22% margin guidance **[FACT]** actually materializing, which implies acceleration through the year off a soft Q1 base. **[INFERENCE]** I hold a constructive-but-conditional view — own it for margin resilience and delivery consistency, not for a demand inflection that has not yet been evidenced. **[SPECULATION]**

## 2. KEY ASSUMPTIONS

- **Growth is guided, not yet demonstrated.** The 3–4% CC FY25 target **[FACT]** requires sequential acceleration, because ~3.7% reported YoY in Q1 **[INFERENCE]** is a rupee figure and CC growth for the quarter is not in the provided set. I assume the reported number is broadly consistent with the low end of guidance, not proof of the high end. **[INFERENCE]**
- **Margin holds inside 20–22%.** Q1 operating margin ~20.8% (derived from ~₹8,183 cr operating profit on ₹39,315 cr) sits at the bottom of the guided band **[INFERENCE]**, so the year needs incremental margin levers (utilization, pyramid, pricing discipline) to reach the mid/upper band. **[SPECULATION]**
- **Non-operating income is real but not the engine.** Total income (₹40,153 cr) exceeds revenue from operations (₹39,315 cr) by ₹838 cr **[FACT]**; I treat that gap as other/treasury income supporting PBT, not as core-demand quality. **[INFERENCE]**
- **Tax rate is normalized.** PBT ₹9,021 cr → PAT ₹6,374 cr implies an effective tax rate of ~29.3% **[INFERENCE]**; I assume no one-off tax item distorts the print absent disclosure. **[INFERENCE]**
- **Base effects flatter the optics.** Q1 FY24 (₹37,933 cr) and Q4 FY24 (₹37,923 cr) were essentially flat **[FACT]**, so even low-single-digit Q1 FY25 growth reads as a genuine sequential restart off a stagnant base. **[INFERENCE]**

## 3. MANAGEMENT COMMITMENTS TO TRACK (checkable next quarter)

- **FY25 revenue growth of 3–4% constant currency** — track whether the FY25 CC guidance is reaffirmed, raised, or cut at Q2 FY25. **[FACT — commitment]**
- **FY25 operating margin of 20–22%** — track whether the band is held and where within it Q2 actually lands versus Q1's ~20.8%. **[FACT — commitment]**
- **Sequential acceleration** — for 3–4% CC to hold, later quarters must out-grow Q1; Q2 CC sequential growth is the first checkpoint. **[INFERENCE from FACT]**
- **Margin trajectory toward the mid-band** — whether margin moves off the ~20.8% floor toward 21%+ is the concrete test of the margin story. **[INFERENCE]**

## 4. RISKS

- **Guidance is back-half loaded.** If Q2 does not show CC acceleration, the 3–4% FY25 target implies an increasingly steep H2, raising cut risk. **[INFERENCE]**
- **Margin at the floor.** Starting the year at the bottom of the 20–22% band leaves little cushion; wage revisions, lower utilization, or pricing pressure could push margin below guidance. **[INFERENCE/SPECULATION]**
- **Reported vs CC ambiguity.** The provided set gives rupee revenue but not Q1 CC growth or segment/geo/vertical detail, so demand quality (discretionary vs cost-takeout, BFSI/retail softness) is unverifiable from these facts. **[FACT — data gap]**
- **Non-operating dependence.** The ₹838 cr gap between total income and operating revenue **[FACT]** means treasury/other income supports reported profit; a swing there would hit PAT without any change in core demand. **[INFERENCE]**
- **Macro/discretionary demand.** Any deterioration in client tech-spend appetite directly threatens the guided restart; not observable in this single print. **[SPECULATION]**

## 5. OBSERVABLE FALSIFIERS (would break the thesis)

- **FY25 CC growth guidance cut below 3%** at any FY25 quarter. **[breaks growth-restart thesis]**
- **Operating margin printing below 20%** in any FY25 quarter, or the 20–22% band being withdrawn. **[breaks margin-anchor thesis]**
- **Q2 FY25 CC sequential growth flat or negative**, invalidating the required acceleration. **[breaks the "modest restart" read]**
- **Reported revenue declining QoQ** in Q2 FY25 (below ₹39,315 cr) absent a disclosed currency/scope reason. **[direct contradiction of restart]**
- **Effective tax rate or other-income normalization** revealing that Q1 PAT (₹6,374 cr) was flattered by a one-off, i.e., core margin materially weaker than ~20.8%. **[breaks the quality-of-earnings assumption]**

## 6. OPEN QUESTIONS

- What was Q1 FY25 **constant-currency** growth (QoQ and YoY), as distinct from the ~3.7% reported rupee figures? Not in the provided set.
- **Segment/vertical/geography** mix and discretionary vs. cost-takeout split — where is the growth coming from?
- **Large-deal TCV and book-to-bill** for the quarter — the leading indicator for whether 3–4% CC is deliverable.
- **Headcount, utilization, and attrition** — the mechanics behind holding/expanding margin off the ~20.8% floor.
- **Composition of the ₹838 cr** non-operating income and its sustainability.
- **Capital return** (buyback/dividend) posture for FY25 — not in the provided set.

## 7. CONFIDENCE

- **§1 Thesis — MEDIUM.** Direction (modest restart + margin anchor) is well supported by the disclosed figures, but the constructive tilt leans on guidance that Q1 does not yet prove.
- **§2 Key Assumptions — MEDIUM.** Arithmetic (growth %, tax rate, income gap, margin) is solid; the forward assumptions (acceleration, margin levers) are reasoned, not disclosed.
- **§3 Commitments to Track — HIGH.** The 3–4% CC and 20–22% margin targets are explicitly stated and cleanly checkable next quarter.
- **§4 Risks — MEDIUM.** Risks follow logically from the numbers and data gaps, but their magnitude is unquantifiable from a single print.
- **§5 Falsifiers — HIGH.** Each is concrete, observable, and tied to a disclosed metric or its next-quarter successor.
