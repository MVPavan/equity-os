- **R4 Measurement semantics.**
  - **Instant.** The instant is the filing's shareholding date (the XBRL context instant). It
    must equal the listing row's date, otherwise `PARSE_FAILED`. A non-quarter-end filing is its
    own instant, and the shareholding run path never calls `fiscal_periods` (R6). Knowledge time
    is the listing's broadcast time (Asia/Kolkata).
  - **Basis and framework (run boundary).** The filing declares neither. A `SHAREHOLDING` package
    is stored `STANDALONE` with framework `UNKNOWN`, and a shareholding run scope must be bases
    `(STANDALONE,)`, otherwise refused. Dictionary rows keep `[CONSOLIDATED, STANDALONE]`
    unchanged. All sources share this pair, so it never discriminates between them.
  - **Applicability.** v3 appends `INSTANT` to `shareholding.every_period.required_in`.
    `ApplicabilityContext` accepts `INSTANT` only with `DocumentType.SHAREHOLDING_PATTERN`. No
    context decidable before changes its decision, because `INSTANT` was refused before.
  - **Hierarchy.** v3 appends SEBI-format members with complete parents, and reuses no v2
    member:
    - `sebi_promoter_group` (A)
    - `public_shareholding` (B), with `institutions_domestic` (B1), `institutions_foreign` (B2),
      `central_state_government` (B3) and `non_institutions` (B4) under it
    - `non_promoter_non_public` (C), with `custodian_dr_holders` (C1) and
      `employee_benefit_trust` (C2) under it
    Names are final in Task 3 from the probe's table labels. The v2 members stay declared and
    unused by v7. Subcategories are not added (Out of scope).
  - **R4 Hierarchy — evidence.** Every parent-child relationship cites a retained applicable regulatory-format or taxonomy relationship definition. Share-count identities are consistency checks only. An unobserved member may be declared as reserved, but receives no selector or fabricated occurrence.
  - **Denominator.** Each first-party percentage takes the denominator its element or format
    heading states, cited in the probe record. A vendor percentage takes `TOTAL_SHARES` or
    `VOTING_RIGHTS` only when a retained structure or definition states it (Upstox's
    documentation, a vendor column heading). Where nothing retained distinguishes the two, the
    label is refused (`UNMAPPED`); equal numbers never decide it.
  - **R4 Denominator — clarification.** A shared percentage caption and a single-denominator-per-table premise do not establish the denominator. Arithmetic elimination cannot establish it. Screener and Tijori percentage labels in the retained probe remain UNMAPPED.
  - **Share class.** New `MemberSource.SECURITY_ISIN` fills the `share_class` axis from the
    occurrence's `native_context["security_isin"]`. The first-party adapter sets it from the
    filing's ISIN after the identity gate. Vendor extractors get it from the API layer, which
    resolves `issuer_values` at the instant and records the identity slice in the context.
    With no single security, or no resolution, nothing is set: the occurrence is keyless and
    never pairs. The member is the ISIN, a public identifier, so it is portable across
    databases.
  - **Identity gate (SHP).** Every context entity identifier (NSE, SEBI and BSE schemes) and every
    ISIN, symbol and scrip identity fact must equal the issuer identifiers held at the instant
    (`issuer_values`); otherwise the submission is `IDENTITY_REJECTED` and nothing is written. The
    adapter imports `IssuerIdentifiers`, the scheme constants and `ACCEPTED_ENTITY_IDS_BY_ISIN`
    but does not call `verify_xbrl_identity`, which requires a results scope.
  - **R4 Share class and identity — probe finding.** The supported instances use the BSE SHP ScripCode scheme. Validate every context identifier and every present filing ISIN, Symbol and ScripCode fact against issuer identifiers held at the instant. The absence of NSE or SEBI schemes is not a failure. Masked ClassOfSecurity supplies no identity evidence. The validated filing ISIN supplies share_class. Listing ISIN is retained as metadata and does not assign or override share_class.
  - **Completeness.** Each occurrence's `disclosure` carries `format_version` (the taxonomy
    identity) and `completeness`. Category totals are `COMPLETE`. Holder lists follow the
    replacement below (it supersedes the earlier holder-list wording).
  - **R4 Completeness — replacement.** Each list records its format and completeness evidence. THRESHOLDED requires retained evidence establishing the threshold and denominator for that list and applicable submission or format. Where this is unavailable, record completeness UNKNOWN, threshold_pct null and threshold_denominator null, with the reason and locator. Neither an unstated threshold nor observed minima establishes COMPLETE or a numerical threshold. Holder observations remain UNMAPPED and keyless; absence never means zero.
  - **R4 Completeness — per list (Sol r5).** From §11.3: public-category and C2 trust lists are `THRESHOLDED` (1, inclusive, `TOTAL_SHARES`); the PAC list is `UNKNOWN`; promoter-group lists are `UNKNOWN` (strict reading of §11.3's two caveats); the present caveated evidence does not make them `COMPLETE`. Vendor lists follow R11. **Acceptance exception (Sol r6 APPROVE, 2026-10-20, narrowly for D8; not settled by the owner decisions):** the D8.2 gate "holder drill-downs are `THRESHOLDED` with the threshold recorded" (roadmap D8 stage 2; data spec §7 and Stage 3 holder gates) is met where retained evidence establishes the list's threshold and denominator, and every other list records `UNKNOWN` with null threshold fields and a retained reason and locator; no threshold is invented. It is an explicit exception to the unconditional holder wording, kept for truthful completeness metadata; it does not claim the universal `THRESHOLDED` condition was shown. Holder absence stays unknown, never zero, and lists stay unmapped.
  - **Vendor month labels.** A label maps to the month-end instant only for Mar, Jun, Sep and Dec.
    Other months are `PERIOD_AMBIGUOUS`: they are kept but not comparable.
- **R5 Pairing (semantics from retained evidence).**
  - **Rule.** Every mapping's denominator and member set rests on retained structural or
    definitional evidence cited in the probe record: an XBRL element or context, a format
    heading, a vendor table's complete row set, or a vendor field definition. Arithmetic over the
    three filings is a consistency check only. A mismatch refutes a ruling, and a match never
    establishes one. A label with more than one surviving alternative is refused (`UNMAPPED`),
    never guessed. Examples: FPI only versus all of B2; B4 versus B4 + C when C is zero; total
    shares versus voting rights.
  - **R5 Unresolved members — clarification.** A member that is absent, zero or below display precision remains semantically unresolved unless retained structure or definition places it. "Unplaced" is evidence metadata only and does not permit a mapping. Retain the probe's E2 refusals.
  - **Denominators.** The denominator is a key dimension, so different denominators are
    different keys and never pair. No new code is needed.
  - **Public.** Each vendor residual label (Screener "Public", Upstox `retail_and_other`, Tijori's
    equivalent) maps to the aggregate its evidence fixes:
    - one filed member, B included, maps directly to it;
    - a sum maps to a v3 derived row per distinct basket, a `DECLARED` dimensional aggregation of
      `shp_holding_pct` over that member list into an output member named for the list. The
      row is family `SHAREHOLDING`, `INSTANT` and of `shp_holding_pct`'s value kind, and its
      aggregated operand has temporal role `CLOSING`, the only role that reads an instant into
      an instant output (`canon/dictionary_validation.py:267-275`). The vendor value is
      published, and the first-party side is computed, as in the Screener "Expenses" precedent.
      Different baskets are different canonical ids and never pair;
    - an unresolved label stays `UNMAPPED`.
    "Retail" is never equated with Public by name.
  - **DII.**
    - A published DII maps to `institutions_domestic` only if its evidence fixes it as B1.
    - The subcategory lines (Upstox `mutual_funds`, `other_dii`) stay `UNMAPPED`, so no key
      carries a subcategory member.
  - **R5 DII — probe ruling.** No DII mapping or construction is evidenced by the retained probe. Task 3 creates none. Construction-only extensions and positive construction tests are omitted from this implementation.
  - **FII.** An FII label maps to `institutions_foreign` only when its evidence fixes B2;
    otherwise it is refused.
  - **Tiers.** Every vendor mapping is `EQUIVALENCE_UNPROVEN`, so outcomes are at best
    `AGREE_UNQUALIFIED` or `ANOMALY` and never trusted; with first-party precision unsupported
    they are `NOT_COMPARABLE` (R10). Upstox pairs are diagnostic, and no precision is invented.
  - **R5 Precision and scale — replacement.** Relevant first-party pure-fraction percentages normalize by x100 exactly once, preserving their original lexemes, units and decimals declarations. The probe's conflicting INF percentage declarations have unsupported precision unless an evidenced scoped precision rule is recorded. Serialized digit counts do not establish precision. Screener and Tijori retain displayed precision; Upstox retains unsupported precision. Missing operands propagate OPERAND_MISSING and are never zero-filled.
- **R6 Run.**
  - `compare/run_family.py` maps each `FilingFamily` to its framework, document type, first-party
    sources and XBRL extractor pin. `FINANCIAL_RESULTS` keeps exactly today's
    `FIRST_PARTY_SOURCES` tuple, `IND_AS`, results document type and results pins.
    `SHAREHOLDING` takes `("NSE_SHP_XBRL",)`, `UNKNOWN`, `SHAREHOLDING_PATTERN` and `nse-shp-v1`,

  - `RunScope.family: FilingFamily = FINANCIAL_RESULTS` is excluded from serialisation at its
    default, so existing identities and replays are unchanged. Source validation becomes
    per family, and shareholding requires bases `(STANDALONE,)`.
  - `request_for(..., family=)` defaults its sources and pins from the table. `load_run_config`
    and replay pick `extractor_xbrl` by family, so ingest, comparison and replay hash the same
    SHP sources. `RunLoader` and the engine read family and framework from the scope.
  - **Group periods and currency.** One engine helper, `_group_periods(entity_id, basis, end)`,
